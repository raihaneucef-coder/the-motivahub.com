I ask because most people download a PDF, skim it, feel productive for about 90 seconds, and never open it again. I've done it myself. It's the most common failure pattern in self-improvement.

The fix is stupidly simple: the worksheet on page 4 takes 8 minutes. You write down one existing habit (your "anchor") and one new tiny behavior you'll stack on top of it. That's it. That's the whole system.

"After I [ANCHOR], I will [NEW HABIT] for [2 minutes or less]."

If you've already done it — great. Tomorrow I'll send you the one thing that separates people who maintain habits from people who white-knuckle for two weeks and quit.
