# Budget Travel: How to See the World Without Going Broke
"Travel is too expensive" is the most common excuse for not exploring the world. But it is just that — an excuse. With the right strategies, you can travel for less than you spend at home.

## How to Travel Cheap

### 1. Travel Off-Season
Flights and hotels are 30-50% cheaper during off-peak seasons. The weather might not be perfect, but your wallet will thank you.

### 2. Use Budget Accommodation
Hostels, guesthouses, and Airbnb are significantly cheaper than hotels. A dorm bed in Southeast Asia costs 5-10€ per night.

### 3. Cook Your Own Meals
Eating out for every meal adds up fast. Cook breakfast and lunch, eat out for dinner. Or shop at local markets and cook in your accommodation.

### 4. Use Public Transportation
Taxis and Uber are expensive. Buses and trains are cheap. Walking is free.

### 5. Travel to Cheap Countries
Southeast Asia, Eastern Europe, Central America, and South America are all significantly cheaper than Western Europe, North America, or Australia.

### 6. Use Travel Rewards
Credit card points, airline miles, and hotel loyalty programs can save you thousands. Use them wisely.

### 7. Travel Slowly
Fewer flights = less money spent on transportation. Stay longer in one place.

## Sample Budget: 1 Month in Southeast Asia

- Accommodation: 300€ (10€/night)
- Food: 250€ (8€/day)
- Transportation: 150€
- Activities: 200€
- **Total: 900€ for a month**

That is less than many people spend on rent at home.
