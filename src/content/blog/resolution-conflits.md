Conflict is not the enemy of relationships. Contempt is. When you fight fairly, conflict can actually strengthen your bond. When you fight unfairly, it destroys trust.

## The 4 Horsemen of Relationship Apocalypse

### 1. Criticism
Attacking the person instead of the behavior. "You never help" is criticism. "I need more help with dishes" is a complaint.

### 2. Contempt
Treating the other person with disrespect. Sarcasm, mockery, eye-rolling, and name-calling are all contempt. It is the single greatest predictor of divorce.

### 3. Defensiveness
Deflecting responsibility. "It's not my fault" or "You do it too" prevents resolution.

### 4. Stonewalling
Shutting down. Walking away. Giving the silent treatment. This is the relationship killer.

## How to Fight Fair

### 1. Use "I" Statements
"I feel hurt when..." instead of "You always..." This takes ownership without blaming.

### 2. Stay on Topic
Do not bring up past grievances. Focus on the current issue. One argument at a time.

### 3. Take Breaks
If emotions run too high, take a 20-minute break. Come back when you are calm. Never fight when you are flooded with emotion.

### 4. Listen to Understand
Your goal is not to win. Your goal is to understand. When you understand the other person's perspective, resolution becomes possible.

### 5. Repair Attempts
Humor, affection, or an apology during conflict can de-escalate tension. The willingness to repair matters more than the conflict itself.
