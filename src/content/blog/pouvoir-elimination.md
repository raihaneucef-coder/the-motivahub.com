<div data-i18n-html="article_pouvoir-elimination_p2">Productivity is not about doing more things. It is about doing fewer things — but the right ones. Learn the art of strategic elimination.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_pouvoir-elimination_p3">We live in a culture that celebrates busyness. More tasks, more meetings, more emails, more everything. But the most productive people do not do more — they do less.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_pouvoir-elimination_p4">They eliminate ruthlessly. They focus only on what matters. And the results speak for themselves.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_pouvoir-elimination_p5"><h2>The Pareto Principle</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_pouvoir-elimination_p6">The 80/20 rule states that 80% of your results come from 20% of your efforts. This means most of what you do does not matter.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_pouvoir-elimination_p7">- 80% of your meetings are unnecessary
- 80% of your emails do not require a response
- 80% of your tasks do not move you toward your goals</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_pouvoir-elimination_p8">The question is: which 20% actually matters?</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_pouvoir-elimination_p9"><h2>How to Eliminate</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_pouvoir-elimination_p10"><h3>1. Audit Your Time</h3>
For one week, track how you spend every hour. You will be shocked at how much time goes to low-value activities.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_pouvoir-elimination_p11"><h3>2. Apply the 80/20 Rule</h3>
Identify the 20% of activities that produce 80% of your results. Focus there. Eliminate or delegate the rest.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_pouvoir-elimination_p12"><h3>3. Learn to Say No</h3>
Every yes is a no to something else. Say no to meetings that could be emails. Say no to projects that do not align with your goals. Say no to requests that drain your energy.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_pouvoir-elimination_p13"><h3>4. Automate or Delegate</h3>
If a task does not require your unique skills, automate it or delegate it. Your time is best spent on high-value work.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_pouvoir-elimination_p14"><h3>5. Embrace Boredom</h3>
When you eliminate distractions, you might feel bored. That is good. Boredom is where creativity lives.</div>
