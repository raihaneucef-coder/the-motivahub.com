<div data-i18n-html="article_langage-corps-confiance_p4"><h2>Power Postures</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_langage-corps-confiance_p5"><h3>The Victory Pose</h3>
Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, hands on your hips, chin slightly raised. Hold for 2 minutes. Studies show this increases testosterone by 20% and decreases cortisol by 25%.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_langage-corps-confiance_p6"><h3>The Confident Walk</h3>
Walk with your shoulders back, head up, and arms swinging naturally. Take up space. Do not shrink yourself.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_langage-corps-confiance_p7"><h3>The Handshake</h3>
A firm (not crushing) handshake with eye contact and a genuine smile projects confidence instantly.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_langage-corps-confiance_p8"><h2>Eye Contact</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_langage-corps-confiance_p9">- Hold eye contact for 3-5 seconds, then look away naturally
- When listening, maintain steady eye contact
- When speaking, look at different people in the group
- Avoid staring — it is intimidating, not confident</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_langage-corps-confiance_p10"><h2>Voice</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_langage-corps-confiance_p11">- Speak slowly and clearly
- Lower your pitch slightly
- Pause before answering important questions
- Eliminate filler words like "um," "uh," "like"</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_langage-corps-confiance_p12"><h2>What to Avoid</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_langage-corps-confiance_p13">- Crossed arms (defensive)
- Fidgeting (nervous)
- Looking at your phone (disrespectful)
- Slouching (unconfident)
- Touching your face (anxious)</div>
