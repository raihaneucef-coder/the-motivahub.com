# Body Language Secrets: How to Project Confidence Without Saying a Word
Research shows that 55% of communication is body language, 38% is tone of voice, and only 7% is actual words. Your body is already speaking — make sure it is saying the right things.

## Power Postures

### The Victory Pose
Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, hands on your hips, chin slightly raised. Hold for 2 minutes. Studies show this increases testosterone by 20% and decreases cortisol by 25%.

### The Confident Walk
Walk with your shoulders back, head up, and arms swinging naturally. Take up space. Do not shrink yourself.

### The Handshake
A firm (not crushing) handshake with eye contact and a genuine smile projects confidence instantly.

## Eye Contact

- Hold eye contact for 3-5 seconds, then look away naturally
- When listening, maintain steady eye contact
- When speaking, look at different people in the group
- Avoid staring — it is intimidating, not confident

## Voice

- Speak slowly and clearly
- Lower your pitch slightly
- Pause before answering important questions
- Eliminate filler words like "um," "uh," "like"

## What to Avoid

- Crossed arms (defensive)
- Fidgeting (nervous)
- Looking at your phone (disrespectful)
- Slouching (unconfident)
- Touching your face (anxious)
