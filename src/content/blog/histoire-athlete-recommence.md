# The Athlete Who Lost Everything and Started Over
She was at the top of her sport. Gold medals, sponsorships, magazine covers. Then the injury hit. Surgery after surgery. The medals stopped. The sponsors left. The magazines moved on.

At 28, she had nothing. She moved back in with her parents. She worked at a coffee shop. She felt like her life was over.

## The Turning Point

One morning, she looked in the mirror and asked: "Is this who I am now?" The answer was no. She was not her medals. She was not her sponsorships. She was not her reputation. She was the person who earned those things — and that person was still inside her.

She started training again. Not for medals. Not for sponsors. For herself.

## The Comeback

Two years later, she competed again. She did not win. She finished 12th. But she cried tears of joy. Because she had proven something to herself: she could start over. She could rebuild. She was more than her worst moment.

## The Lesson

You are not your failures. You are not your setbacks. You are the person who chooses to get back up. And that person is unstoppable.

> *It is not how many times you fall. It is how many times you get up. — Vince Lombardi*
