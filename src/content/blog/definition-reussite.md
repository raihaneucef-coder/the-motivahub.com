# The Definition of Success Is Personal: Stop Copying Others
The biggest mistake you can make is chasing someone else's version of success. A Ferrari does not make you successful if you hate driving. A corner office does not make you successful if you hate your job.

## Why We Copy Others

### 1. Social Conditioning
From childhood, we are taught that success looks a certain way: money, status, possessions. But this is someone else's definition.

### 2. Comparison Trap
Social media shows us highlight reels. We compare our behind-the-scenes to everyone else's best moments.

### 3. Fear of Being Different
It takes courage to define success on your own terms. It is easier to follow the crowd.

## How to Define Your Success

### 1. Ask the Right Questions
- What makes you feel alive?
- What would you do if money were not a factor?
- What do you want to be remembered for?
- What does a perfect day look like to you?

### 2. Write Your Definition
Write a paragraph describing what success means to you. Not what your parents want. Not what society expects. What YOU want.

### 3. Align Your Life With It
Once you know what success means to you, align your daily actions with that definition. This is where fulfillment comes from.

## The Real Secret

The most successful people are not the richest. They are the most fulfilled. And fulfillment comes from living in alignment with your own values — not someone else's.

> *Success is not the key to happiness. Happiness is the key to success. — Albert Schweitzer*
