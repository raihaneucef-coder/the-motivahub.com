# Energy Management: The Real Productivity Hack
You can have 8 hours in a day and waste every one of them if your energy is low. You can have 2 hours and accomplish more than most people do in a full day — if your energy is high.

Time management is not enough. You need energy management.

## The Four Dimensions of Energy

### 1. Physical Energy
Your body is the foundation of your energy. Without physical energy, everything else suffers.

- Sleep 7-8 hours (non-negotiable)
- Exercise daily (even a 20-minute walk)
- Eat for performance (protein, vegetables, water)
- Take breaks between work blocks

### 2. Emotional Energy
Negative emotions drain energy. Positive emotions fuel it.

- Spend time with people who lift you up
- Practice gratitude daily
- Set boundaries with energy vampires
- Do things that bring you joy

### 3. Mental Energy
Focus requires mental energy. When your mental energy is low, you cannot concentrate.

- Take breaks to recharge
- Avoid multitasking
- Practice mindfulness or meditation
- Single-task for better results

### 4. Spiritual Energy
Purpose fuels persistence. When you know why you are doing something, you find the energy to keep going.

- Connect your work to a larger purpose
- Set meaningful goals
- Align your actions with your values
- Celebrate progress, not just outcomes

## Energy-Boosting Strategies

### Morning Energy
- Hydrate immediately upon waking
- Move your body for 10 minutes
- Eat a protein-rich breakfast
- Start with your most important task

### Afternoon Energy
- Take a 20-minute power nap
- Go for a short walk
- Eat a light lunch
- Switch to easier tasks

### Evening Energy
- Wind down with a routine
- Avoid screens before bed
- Reflect on the day
- Prepare for tomorrow

> *Energy, not time, is the fundamental currency of high performance. — Tony Schwartz*
