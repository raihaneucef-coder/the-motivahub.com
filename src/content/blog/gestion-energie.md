<div data-i18n-html="article_gestion-energie_p3"><h1>Energy Management: The Real Productivity Hack</h1>
You can have 8 hours in a day and waste every one of them if your energy is low. You can have 2 hours and accomplish more than most people do in a full day — if your energy is high.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_gestion-energie_p4">Time management is not enough. You need energy management.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_gestion-energie_p5"><h2>The Four Dimensions of Energy</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_gestion-energie_p6"><h3>1. Physical Energy</h3>
Your body is the foundation of your energy. Without physical energy, everything else suffers.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_gestion-energie_p7">- Sleep 7-8 hours (non-negotiable)
- Exercise daily (even a 20-minute walk)
- Eat for performance (protein, vegetables, water)
- Take breaks between work blocks</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_gestion-energie_p8"><h3>2. Emotional Energy</h3>
Negative emotions drain energy. Positive emotions fuel it.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_gestion-energie_p9">- Spend time with people who lift you up
- Practice gratitude daily
- Set boundaries with energy vampires
- Do things that bring you joy</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_gestion-energie_p10"><h3>3. Mental Energy</h3>
Focus requires mental energy. When your mental energy is low, you cannot concentrate.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_gestion-energie_p11">- Take breaks to recharge
- Avoid multitasking
- Practice mindfulness or meditation
- Single-task for better results</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_gestion-energie_p12"><h3>4. Spiritual Energy</h3>
Purpose fuels persistence. When you know why you are doing something, you find the energy to keep going.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_gestion-energie_p13">- Connect your work to a larger purpose
- Set meaningful goals
- Align your actions with your values
- Celebrate progress, not just outcomes</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_gestion-energie_p14"><h2>Energy-Boosting Strategies</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_gestion-energie_p15"><h3>Morning Energy</h3>
- Hydrate immediately upon waking
- Move your body for 10 minutes
- Eat a protein-rich breakfast
- Start with your most important task</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_gestion-energie_p16"><h3>Afternoon Energy</h3>
- Take a 20-minute power nap
- Go for a short walk
- Eat a light lunch
- Switch to easier tasks</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_gestion-energie_p17"><h3>Evening Energy</h3>
- Wind down with a routine
- Avoid screens before bed
- Reflect on the day
- Prepare for tomorrow</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_gestion-energie_p18"><blockquote><em>Energy, not time, is the fundamental currency of high performance. — Tony Schwartz</em></blockquote></div>
