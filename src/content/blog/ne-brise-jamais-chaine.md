<div data-i18n-html="article_ne-brise-jamais-chaine_p2"><strong>Jerry Seinfeld's habit strategy:</strong> mark an X on a calendar every day you complete your habit, and never break the chain. It works because of loss aversion, visual proof, and momentum. The longer the chain, the harder it is to break. The practical version includes the 2-day rule: never miss twice, not never miss once.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_ne-brise-jamais-chaine_p3">Jerry Seinfeld was asked how he became a better comedian. His answer was one sentence: "Write a joke every day. When you do, put a big red X on a calendar. After a few days, you will have a chain. Do not break the chain."</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_ne-brise-jamais-chaine_p5"><h2>The Power of the Chain</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_ne-brise-jamais-chaine_p6">The chain is a visual representation of your consistency. Every day you complete your habit, you add a link. Every link makes the chain stronger. And once the chain is long enough, you will do anything to protect it.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_ne-brise-jamais-chaine_p7">The chain works because of three psychological principles:
1. <strong>Loss aversion:</strong> You hate losing what you have built
2. <strong>Visual proof:</strong> You can see your progress
3. <strong>Momentum:</strong> The longer the chain, the harder it is to stop</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_ne-brise-jamais-chaine_p8"><h2>How to Use the Chain</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_ne-brise-jamais-chaine_p9"><h3>1. Choose Your Habit</h3>
Pick one habit. Not three. Not five. One. The habit that matters most right now.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_ne-brise-jamais-chaine_p10"><h3>2. Get a Tracker</h3>
Use a calendar, a habit tracker app, or our <a href="/tracker/">Habit Tracker</a>. The format does not matter. The consistency does.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_ne-brise-jamais-chaine_p11"><h3>3. Mark Every Day</h3>
Every day you complete the habit, mark it. Put an X. Check a box. Add a gold star. The act of marking is its own reward.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_ne-brise-jamais-chaine_p12"><h3>4. Never Miss Twice</h3>
You will miss a day. Everyone does. The rule is simple: <strong>never miss twice</strong>. One missed day is a accident. Two missed days is the beginning of a new habit — the habit of quitting.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_ne-brise-jamais-chaine_p13"><h3>5. Protect the Chain</h3>
After 7 days, the chain becomes precious. After 30 days, it becomes part of your identity. After 90 days, it becomes who you are. Protect it like you would protect something valuable — because it is.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_ne-brise-jamais-chaine_p14"><h2>The Math of Consistency</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_ne-brise-jamais-chaine_p15">If you practice a habit 90% of the time for one year, you will practice it 328 days. That is 328 votes for your new identity. That is 328 days of compound growth. That is a person who is fundamentally different from the person who started.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_ne-brise-jamais-chaine_p16">If you practice 50% of the time, you practice 182 days. Still good. But the gap between 90% and 50% is massive over a year.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_ne-brise-jamais-chaine_p17">The chain pushes you toward 100%. Not because you have to be perfect. But because every mark feels too valuable to lose.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_ne-brise-jamais-chaine_p18"><h2>Real-World Chains</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_ne-brise-jamais-chaine_p19"><strong>Writing:</strong> Write 500 words every day. Mark it. After 30 days, you have 15,000 words. After 90 days, you have a book draft.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_ne-brise-jamais-chaine_p20"><strong>Exercise:</strong> Move for 20 minutes every day. Mark it. After 30 days, you have exercised for 10 hours. After 90 days, you have a body that feels different.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_ne-brise-jamais-chaine_p21"><strong>Reading:</strong> Read 10 pages every day. Mark it. After 30 days, you have read a book. After 90 days, you have read three books.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_ne-brise-jamais-chaine_p22"><strong>Meditation:</strong> Sit for 5 minutes every day. Mark it. After 30 days, you have meditated for 2.5 hours. After 90 days, you have a calmer mind.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_ne-brise-jamais-chaine_p23"><h2>What Happens When You Break the Chain</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_ne-brise-jamais-chaine_p24">You will break the chain eventually. Maybe you get sick. Maybe you travel. Maybe life happens.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_ne-brise-jamais-chaine_p25">Do not panic. Do not quit. Just start again the next day. The chain is not about perfection. It is about <strong>recovery speed</strong>.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_ne-brise-jamais-chaine_p26">The people who succeed are not the ones who never break the chain. They are the ones who start again immediately after breaking it.</div>
