# Digital Detox: How Screen Time Is Destroying Your Health
Your phone is the first thing you touch in the morning and the last thing you touch at night. It is also destroying your health — and you do not even realize it.

## The Effects of Excessive Screen Time

### 1. Sleep Disruption
Blue light from screens suppresses melatonin production. This makes it harder to fall asleep and reduces sleep quality.

### 2. Reduced Focus
Constant notifications train your brain to expect interruptions. This makes deep work nearly impossible.

### 3. Increased Anxiety
Social media comparison, news cycles, and information overload increase anxiety and depression.

### 4. Physical Pain
Hunching over phones causes neck pain, back pain, and eye strain. "Text neck" is a real medical condition.

### 5. Reduced Social Skills
When you spend more time on screens than with people, your social skills deteriorate.

## How to Detox

### 1. Set Screen-Free Times
No screens for the first hour after waking and the last hour before bed. This alone transforms your sleep and morning energy.

### 2. Turn Off Notifications
Keep only the essential notifications. Everything else is a distraction.

### 3. Use App Timers
Set daily limits for social media apps. When the timer is up, put the phone down.

### 4. Create Phone-Free Zones
The bedroom and dining table are good places to start. These spaces should be for rest and connection — not screens.

### 5. Replace Screen Time
Do not just remove screen time — replace it. Read, walk, exercise, or spend time with people you love.
