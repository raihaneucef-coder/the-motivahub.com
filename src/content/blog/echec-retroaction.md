<div data-i18n-html="article_echec-retroaction_p3"><h1>Failure Is Feedback: How to Use Setbacks as Stepping Stones</h1>
Thomas Edison failed 10,000 times before inventing the lightbulb. When asked about his failures, he said: "I have not failed. I have just found 10,000 ways that will not work."</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_echec-retroaction_p4">Failure is not the opposite of success. It is part of success.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_echec-retroaction_p5"><h2>Why Failure Is Valuable</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_echec-retroaction_p6"><h3>1. It Teaches What Does Not Work</h3>
Every failure eliminates an approach that does not work. This brings you closer to the approach that does.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_echec-retroaction_p7"><h3>2. It Builds Resilience</h3>
The more you fail, the more comfortable you become with failure. This makes you bolder, more creative, and more willing to take risks.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_echec-retroaction_p8"><h3>3. It Reveals Character</h3>
Success does not reveal character. Failure does. How you handle failure determines who you truly are.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_echec-retroaction_p9"><h3>4. It Creates Empathy</h3>
People who have failed understand struggle. They are more compassionate, more relatable, and more effective leaders.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_echec-retroaction_p10"><h2>How to Use Failure</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_echec-retroaction_p11"><h3>1. Reframe It</h3>
Failure is not "I am a failure." Failure is "this approach did not work." Separate the outcome from your identity.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_echec-retroaction_p12"><h3>2. Extract the Lesson</h3>
Ask: "What did I learn?" Every failure has a lesson. Find it.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_echec-retroaction_p13"><h3>3. Adjust and Try Again</h3>
Failure without adjustment is just failure. Failure with adjustment is experimentation.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_echec-retroaction_p14"><h3>4. Share Your Failures</h3>
When you share your failures, you inspire others. You also learn from the failures of others.</div>
