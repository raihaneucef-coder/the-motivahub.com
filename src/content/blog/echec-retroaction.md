Thomas Edison failed 10,000 times before inventing the lightbulb. When asked about his failures, he said: "I have not failed. I have just found 10,000 ways that will not work."

Failure is not the opposite of success. It is part of success.

## Why Failure Is Valuable

### 1. It Teaches What Does Not Work
Every failure eliminates an approach that does not work. This brings you closer to the approach that does.

### 2. It Builds Resilience
The more you fail, the more comfortable you become with failure. This makes you bolder, more creative, and more willing to take risks.

### 3. It Reveals Character
Success does not reveal character. Failure does. How you handle failure determines who you truly are.

### 4. It Creates Empathy
People who have failed understand struggle. They are more compassionate, more relatable, and more effective leaders.

## How to Use Failure

### 1. Reframe It
Failure is not "I am a failure." Failure is "this approach did not work." Separate the outcome from your identity.

### 2. Extract the Lesson
Ask: "What did I learn?" Every failure has a lesson. Find it.

### 3. Adjust and Try Again
Failure without adjustment is just failure. Failure with adjustment is experimentation.

### 4. Share Your Failures
When you share your failures, you inspire others. You also learn from the failures of others.
