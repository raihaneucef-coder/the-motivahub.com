Most people do not budget because they think it is complicated. It is not. The 50/30/20 rule is so simple you can remember it on one hand.

## The Rule

- **50% Needs:** Housing, food, transportation, utilities, insurance
- **30% Wants:** Entertainment, dining out, hobbies, shopping
- **20% Savings/Debt:** Emergency fund, investments, debt repayment

## How It Works

### Example: 3000€ Monthly Income

- **50% Needs = 1500€**
  - Rent: 800€
  - Food: 300€
  - Transportation: 200€
  - Utilities: 200€

- **30% Wants = 900€**
  - Dining out: 200€
  - Entertainment: 200€
  - Shopping: 200€
  - Hobbies: 300€

- **20% Savings = 600€**
  - Emergency fund: 300€
  - Investments: 300€

## Why It Works

### 1. It Is Simple
No complicated spreadsheets. No tracking every penny. Just three categories.

### 2. It Is Flexible
The percentages can be adjusted based on your situation. If you live in an expensive city, your needs might be 60%. Adjust accordingly.

### 3. It Prioritizes Savings
By allocating 20% to savings first, you ensure you are building wealth — not just spending everything you earn.

### 4. It Allows Guilt-Free Spending
The 30% for wants is your "fun money." You can spend it without guilt, knowing your needs and savings are covered.

## How to Start

1. Calculate your after-tax income
2. Categorize your current spending
3. Adjust to fit the 50/30/20 rule
4. Automate savings transfers
5. Review monthly

> *A budget is telling your money where to go instead of wondering where it went. — Dave Ramsey*
