Social media is the most powerful tool ever created — and the most dangerous. It connects you to the world and disconnects you from yourself. The key is using it intentionally.

## The Problem

Social media is designed to be addictive. Every notification, like, and comment triggers a dopamine hit. Your brain craves more. This creates a cycle of dependency that is hard to break.

## How to Use Social Media Intentionally

### 1. Set Time Limits
Use app timers to limit daily usage. 30 minutes per day is enough to stay connected without getting consumed.

### 2. Curate Your Feed
Follow accounts that inspire, educate, or make you laugh. Unfollow accounts that make you feel inadequate, angry, or anxious.

### 3. Post with Purpose
Before posting, ask: "Why am I sharing this?" If the answer is "for validation," reconsider. Post for connection, not for likes.

### 4. Disable Notifications
Turn off all non-essential notifications. You do not need to know every time someone likes your photo.

### 5. Use It, Do Not Let It Use You
Social media is a tool — not a master. Use it to learn, connect, and share. Do not let it use you for attention, data, and addiction.

## The Balance

- Use social media for 30 minutes per day
- Do not use it in the first hour after waking
- Do not use it in the last hour before bed
- Replace scrolling with reading, exercise, or connection

> *Comparison is the thief of joy. — Theodore Roosevelt*
