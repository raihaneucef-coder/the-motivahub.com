<div data-i18n-html="article_fond-urgence_p3"><h1>Emergency Fund: Why It Is Your Financial Foundation</h1>
Your car breaks down. Your roof leaks. You get sick. These are not possibilities — they are certainties. The question is not if they will happen, but when. An emergency fund is what keeps them from becoming financial disasters.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_fond-urgence_p4"><h2>How Much Do You Need?</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_fond-urgence_p5">The standard recommendation is 3-6 months of essential expenses. For most people, that is:
- <strong>Minimum:</strong> 3 months of rent, food, transportation, utilities
- <strong>Ideal:</strong> 6 months of essential expenses
- <strong>Conservative:</strong> 12 months of essential expenses</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_fond-urgence_p6"><h2>How to Build It</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_fond-urgence_p7"><h3>1. Start Small</h3>
You do not need 10,000€ tomorrow. Start with 500€ — enough to cover a minor emergency. Then keep building.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_fond-urgence_p8"><h3>2. Automate It</h3>
Set up an automatic transfer of 10-20% of your income to a separate savings account. Treat it like a bill — non-negotiable.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_fond-urgence_p9"><h3>3. Keep It Separate</h3>
Do not keep your emergency fund in your checking account. Put it in a high-yield savings account where it is accessible but not tempting.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_fond-urgence_p10"><h3>4. Use It Only for Emergencies</h3>
An emergency is not a sale at your favorite store. An emergency is a medical bill, a job loss, or a necessary repair. Not a vacation or a new phone.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_fond-urgence_p11"><h2>Why It Matters</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_fond-urgence_p12">Without an emergency fund, unexpected expenses go on credit cards. Credit cards charge 20%+ interest. One emergency can create a debt spiral that takes years to escape.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_fond-urgence_p13">With an emergency fund, unexpected expenses are inconvenient — not devastating.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_fond-urgence_p14"><blockquote><em>Do not save what is left after spending. Spend what is left after saving. — Warren Buffett</em></blockquote></div>
