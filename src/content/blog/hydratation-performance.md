# Hydration: Why Water Is the Most Underrated Performance Enhancer
You can survive 3 weeks without food. You can only survive 3 days without water. Yet most people walk around chronically dehydrated — and wonder why they feel tired, foggy, and unproductive.

## How Dehydration Affects You

- **2% dehydration** = 10% decrease in strength
- **3% dehydration** = 20% decrease in endurance
- **5% dehydration** = 30% decrease in performance

Even mild dehydration reduces cognitive function, mood, and energy levels.

## How Much Water Do You Need?

The general rule is 35ml per kg of body weight. For a 70kg person, that is 2.45 liters per day. But this varies based on activity level, climate, and individual needs.

A simple test: if your urine is pale yellow, you are hydrated. If it is dark yellow, drink more.

## When to Drink

### 1. First Thing in the Morning
You dehydrate overnight. Drinking 500ml of water first thing rehydrates your body and kickstarts your metabolism.

### 2. Before Meals
Drinking water before meals helps with digestion and can reduce appetite.

### 3. During Exercise
Drink 200-300ml every 15-20 minutes during exercise.

### 4. After Exercise
Replace the fluid you lost. Weigh yourself before and after exercise — drink 1.5ml for every gram lost.

## Tips to Drink More

1. Carry a water bottle everywhere
2. Set hourly reminders
3. Drink a glass of water before every meal
4. Flavor your water with lemon or cucumber
5. Eat water-rich foods (watermelon, cucumber, oranges)
