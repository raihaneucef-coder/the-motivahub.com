We wait for a big moment to change our lives. It never comes that way.

Big lives are not built in big moments. They are built in small wins, repeated so often they become who you are.

**A tiny habit is not a lesser version of a big habit. It is the seed of the big habit.**

Reading one page is not a failed version of reading a book. It is the vote that says: I am a reader. Doing one push-up is not a failed workout. It is the proof that you are someone who moves.

Make it so small you cannot say no:

- **Write one sentence.** Not a page, a sentence. The sentence often becomes a paragraph.
- **Read one page.** Not a chapter, a page. The page often becomes ten minutes.
- **Tidy for two minutes.** Not the whole house, two minutes. The two minutes often becomes the room.

The size is not the point. The win is the point. Each small win is evidence that you are changing. Evidence, repeated, becomes identity. Identity, repeated, becomes a big life.

Do not wait for motivation. Collect a small win today. Big lives are just small wins, remembered.
