<div data-i18n-html="article_regle-1-pourcent_p2">Improve by just 1% per day and the compounding math works out to roughly 37× over a year. That figure is an illustration of momentum, not a literal promise — the real point is that tiny gains add up. The execution requires discipline.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regle-1-pourcent_p3">Get 1% better every day for a year and, by the arithmetic of compounding (1.01³⁶⁵ ≈ 37.8), you finish a long way ahead. Skills don't literally multiply 37×, but consistency beats intensity the way interest beats a lump sum. This is the compound effect, and it is the most powerful force in personal growth.</div>


<div data-i18n-html="article_regle-1-pourcent_p4"><h2>The Math</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regle-1-pourcent_p5">- Day 1: 1.01
- Day 30: 1.35
- Day 60: 1.82
- Day 90: 2.45
- Day 180: 6.04
- Day 365: 37.78</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regle-1-pourcent_p6">One percent does not feel like much. But 1% <a href="/journal/long-ascent/">compounded daily is extraordinary</a>.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regle-1-pourcent_p7"><h2>How to Apply the 1% Rule</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regle-1-pourcent_p8"><h3>1. <a href="/journal/lire-divertissement/">Read 10 Pages Per Day</a></h3>
10 pages per day = 3,650 pages per year = about 18 books. That is more than most people read in a decade.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regle-1-pourcent_p9"><h3>2. Exercise 10 Minutes Per Day</h3>
10 minutes per day = 60 hours per year. That is enough to transform your health.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regle-1-pourcent_p10"><h3>3. Practice a Skill 15 Minutes Per Day</h3>
15 minutes per day = 90 hours per year. That is enough to go from beginner to competent in almost any skill.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regle-1-pourcent_p17">The science behind 'small and daily' is not just arithmetic — it is behavior. BJ Fogg's research on tiny habits at Stanford found that motivation is unreliable, so the winning move is to shrink the action until it feels almost too easy, attaching it to a routine you already have. A 1% step you actually repeat every day beats a 10% plan you abandon by Thursday. The compounding the slogan celebrates is the compounding of showing up, not of heroics.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regle-1-pourcent_p11"><h3>4. Meditate 5 Minutes Per Day</h3>
5 minutes per day = 30 hours per year. That is enough to rewire your brain for calm and focus.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regle-1-pourcent_p13">- It is sustainable — you do not burn out
- It is invisible — you do not notice the change day to day
- It is compounding — the results multiply over time
- It is identity-building — you become the type of person who improves daily</div>
