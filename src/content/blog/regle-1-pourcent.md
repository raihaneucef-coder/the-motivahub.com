<div data-i18n-html="article_regle-1-pourcent_p4"><h2>The Math</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regle-1-pourcent_p5">- Day 1: 1.01
- Day 30: 1.35
- Day 60: 1.82
- Day 90: 2.45
- Day 180: 6.04
- Day 365: 37.78</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regle-1-pourcent_p6">One percent does not feel like much. But 1% compounded daily is extraordinary.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regle-1-pourcent_p7"><h2>How to Apply the 1% Rule</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regle-1-pourcent_p8"><h3>1. Read 10 Pages Per Day</h3>
10 pages per day = 3,650 pages per year = about 18 books. That is more than most people read in a decade.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regle-1-pourcent_p9"><h3>2. Exercise 10 Minutes Per Day</h3>
10 minutes per day = 60 hours per year. That is enough to transform your health.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regle-1-pourcent_p10"><h3>3. Practice a Skill 15 Minutes Per Day</h3>
15 minutes per day = 90 hours per year. That is enough to go from beginner to competent in almost any skill.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regle-1-pourcent_p11"><h3>4. Meditate 5 Minutes Per Day</h3>
5 minutes per day = 30 hours per year. That is enough to rewire your brain for calm and focus.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regle-1-pourcent_p12"><h2>Why 1% Works</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regle-1-pourcent_p13">- It is sustainable — you do not burn out
- It is invisible — you do not notice the change day to day
- It is compounding — the results multiply over time
- It is identity-building — you become the type of person who improves daily</div>
