If you improve by 1% every day for a year, you will be 37 times better by the end of the year. This is the compound effect, and it is the most powerful force in personal growth.

## The Math

- Day 1: 1.01
- Day 30: 1.35
- Day 60: 1.82
- Day 90: 2.45
- Day 180: 6.04
- Day 365: 37.78

One percent does not feel like much. But 1% compounded daily is extraordinary.

## How to Apply the 1% Rule

### 1. Read 10 Pages Per Day
10 pages per day = 3,650 pages per year = about 18 books. That is more than most people read in a decade.

### 2. Exercise 10 Minutes Per Day
10 minutes per day = 60 hours per year. That is enough to transform your health.

### 3. Practice a Skill 15 Minutes Per Day
15 minutes per day = 90 hours per year. That is enough to go from beginner to competent in almost any skill.

### 4. Meditate 5 Minutes Per Day
5 minutes per day = 30 hours per year. That is enough to rewire your brain for calm and focus.

## Why 1% Works

- It is sustainable — you do not burn out
- It is invisible — you do not notice the change day to day
- It is compounding — the results multiply over time
- It is identity-building — you become the type of person who improves daily

> *Small daily improvements are the key to staggering long-term results. — Robin Sharma*
