"If the first thing you do each morning is eat a live frog, you can go through the rest of the day knowing the worst is behind you." — Mark Twain

The frog is your hardest, most important task — the one you are most likely to procrastinate on. Eating it first means doing it before anything else.

## Why the Frog Works

### 1. Willpower Is Finite
Your willpower is highest in the morning. By the afternoon, it is depleted. If you save the hard task for later, you will not have the energy to do it well.

### 2. It Creates Momentum
Completing the hardest task first gives you a sense of accomplishment that carries through the rest of the day. Everything else feels easy by comparison.

### 3. It Eliminates Dread
The frog hangs over your head all day when you avoid it. The anxiety of "I still have to do that" drains energy you could use for other things.

### 4. It Proves You Are Capable
Every time you eat the frog, you prove to yourself that you can do hard things. This builds confidence and self-trust.

## How to Apply It

1. Identify your frog the night before
2. Do it first thing in the morning
3. Do not check email or social media before it is done
4. Celebrate when it is finished

## Real-World Frogs

- Writing a difficult email
- Starting a project you have been postponing
- Having a conversation you have been avoiding
- Studying for an exam
- Exercising when you do not feel like it

> *If you have to eat two frogs, eat the biggest one first. — Mark Twain*
