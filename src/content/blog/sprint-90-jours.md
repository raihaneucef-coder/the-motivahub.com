The 90-Day Sprint: Why Quarterly Goals Beat Annual Goals works when you test it personally, not when you read about it theoretically. This article gives you the exact protocol, the mistakes to avoid, and the evidence behind each step. The goal is not perfection. The goal is progress.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_sprint-90-jours_p3">Annual goals are too easy to postpone. "I will do it later" works when "later" is 10 months away. But when "later" is 90 days away, everything changes.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_sprint-90-jours_p4">The 90-day sprint is the most effective goal-setting timeframe. It is long enough to accomplish something meaningful — and short enough to create urgency.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_sprint-90-jours_p5"><h2>Why 90 Days Works</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_sprint-90-jours_p6"><h3>1. Urgency</h3>
90 days is close enough to feel real. You cannot afford to procrastinate when the deadline is three months away, not twelve.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_sprint-90-jours_p7"><h3>2. Focus</h3>
With only 90 days, you cannot pursue 10 goals. You must choose 2-3 that matter most. This forced focus is a gift.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_sprint-90-jours_p8"><h3>3. Momentum</h3>
90 days is long enough to build momentum, but short enough to see results before motivation fades.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_sprint-90-jours_p9"><h3>4. Adaptability</h3>
The world changes fast. A 90-day goal allows you to adjust every quarter based on what you have learned.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_sprint-90-jours_p10"><h2>How to Run a 90-Day Sprint</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_sprint-90-jours_p11"><h3>Week 1: Plan</h3>
- Define 2-3 goals for the next 90 days
- Break each goal into weekly milestones
- Identify the daily actions required</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_sprint-90-jours_p12"><h3>Weeks 2-11: Execute</h3>
- Follow your daily actions without fail
- Review progress weekly
- Adjust the plan when needed — not the goal</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_sprint-90-jours_p13"><h3>Week 12: Review</h3>
- Did you achieve the goals?
- What worked? What did not?
- What will you do differently next quarter?</div>
