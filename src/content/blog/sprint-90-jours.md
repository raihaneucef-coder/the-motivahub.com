Annual goals are too easy to postpone. "I will do it later" works when "later" is 10 months away. But when "later" is 90 days away, everything changes.

The 90-day sprint is the most effective goal-setting timeframe. It is long enough to accomplish something meaningful — and short enough to create urgency.

## Why 90 Days Works

### 1. Urgency
90 days is close enough to feel real. You cannot afford to procrastinate when the deadline is three months away, not twelve.

### 2. Focus
With only 90 days, you cannot pursue 10 goals. You must choose 2-3 that matter most. This forced focus is a gift.

### 3. Momentum
90 days is long enough to build momentum, but short enough to see results before motivation fades.

### 4. Adaptability
The world changes fast. A 90-day goal allows you to adjust every quarter based on what you have learned.

## How to Run a 90-Day Sprint

### Week 1: Plan
- Define 2-3 goals for the next 90 days
- Break each goal into weekly milestones
- Identify the daily actions required

### Weeks 2-11: Execute
- Follow your daily actions without fail
- Review progress weekly
- Adjust the plan when needed — not the goal

### Week 12: Review
- Did you achieve the goals?
- What worked? What did not?
- What will you do differently next quarter?

## Real-World 90-Day Goals

- **Fitness:** "Run a 10K in 90 days"
- **Finance:** "Save 3000€ in 90 days"
- **Career:** "Complete the certification in 90 days"
- **Learning:** "Finish the course in 90 days"
- **Writing:** "Write 30 articles in 90 days"

> *The secret to getting ahead is getting started. The secret to getting started is breaking your complex, overwhelming tasks into small manageable tasks, and starting on the first one. — Mark Twain*
