Relationships do not need grand gestures. They need small, consistent acts of attention. The 5-minute daily habit is simple: spend 5 minutes giving your full, undivided attention to the person you love.

## How It Works

### 1. Put Your Phone Away
Not on silent. Not face-down. Away. In another room. Your phone is the enemy of connection.

### 2. Make Eye Contact
Look at the person. Not at the TV. Not at the wall. At them. Eye contact is the simplest form of intimacy.

### 3. Ask a Real Question
Not "How was your day?" That gets a one-word answer. Ask: "What was the best part of your day?" or "What are you thinking about?"

### 4. Listen Without Fixing
When they talk, listen. Do not offer solutions unless they ask. Most people do not want advice — they want to be heard.

### 5. Express Gratitude
End the 5 minutes with one specific thing you appreciate about them. "Thank you for making dinner tonight. It meant a lot."

## Why It Works

The 5-minute habit works because it addresses the number one reason relationships fail: feeling unseen. When you give someone your full attention for 5 minutes, you are saying: "You matter. I see you. You are important to me."

That is all most people need.

> *The most important thing in communication is hearing what isn't said. — Peter Drucker*
