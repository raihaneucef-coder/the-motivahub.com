<div data-i18n-html="article_regles-goggins-mental_p3"><h1>David Goggins' 5 Rules for an Unbreakable Mind</h1>
David Goggins was 300 pounds, working a low-paying job, and living a life that did not reflect his potential. Four years later, he was a Navy SEAL, an ultramarathon runner, and one of the toughest humans alive.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regles-goggins-mental_p4">His transformation was not powered by motivation. It was powered by five cold rules.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regles-goggins-mental_p5"><h2>Rule 1: The 40% Rule</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regles-goggins-mental_p6">When your mind tells you that you are done — that you cannot take another step, another rep, another minute — you are only at 40% of your capacity.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regles-goggins-mental_p7">Your brain has a governor. It shuts you down early to protect you from discomfort. But that discomfort is not danger — it is growth.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regles-goggins-mental_p8"><strong>How to apply it:</strong> The next time you want to quit, tell yourself: "I am only at 40%." Then do 10% more. You will be amazed at what your body and mind can handle.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regles-goggins-mental_p9"><h2>Rule 2: Take Souls</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regles-goggins-mental_p10">This is not about dominating others. It is about <strong>outworking everyone</strong> so completely that your effort inspires others to raise their standards.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regles-goggins-mental_p11">When you show up with relentless discipline, you do not just improve yourself. You improve everyone around you. Your effort becomes contagious.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regles-goggins-mental_p12"><strong>How to apply it:</strong> Outwork the person next to you. Not with arrogance — with quiet, relentless effort. Let your actions speak.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regles-goggins-mental_p13"><h2>Rule 3: Cookie Jar</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regles-goggins-mental_p14">The Cookie Jar is your mental collection of past victories. When you face a new challenge, reach into the Cookie Jar and remember every hard thing you have already overcome.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regles-goggins-mental_p15">- You survived that breakup
- You passed that exam
- You finished that project
- You got through that injury</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regles-goggins-mental_p16">You have already proven you can do hard things. This challenge is no different.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regles-goggins-mental_p17"><strong>How to apply it:</strong> Write down your 10 greatest achievements. When doubt arises, read the list. You have evidence that you are capable.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regles-goggins-mental_p18"><h2>Rule 4: Accountability Mirror</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regles-goggins-mental_p19">Every morning, look in the mirror and tell yourself the truth. Not the comfortable truth — the real truth.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regles-goggins-mental_p20">- "I am overweight, and I need to fix it"
- "I am lazy, and I need to change"
- "I am wasting my potential, and I need to act"</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regles-goggins-mental_p21">This is not self-hatred. It is self-honesty. And self-honesty is the foundation of change.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regles-goggins-mental_p22"><strong>How to apply it:</strong> Every morning, look in the mirror and say one thing you need to improve. Then take one action to improve it.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regles-goggins-mental_p23"><h2>Rule 5: The Callus Mind</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regles-goggins-mental_p24">A callus forms on your hand when you repeated friction. The same thing happens in your mind. When you repeatedly expose yourself to discomfort, your mind hardens.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regles-goggins-mental_p25">- Take cold showers daily
- Do the thing you are afraid of
- Embrace the suck</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regles-goggins-mental_p26">Over time, what once felt unbearable becomes routine. Your mind develops calluses. And a mind with calluses is unbreakable.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regles-goggins-mental_p27"><strong>How to apply it:</strong> Do one uncomfortable thing every day. Start small. Cold shower for 30 seconds. Talk to a stranger. Wake up 15 minutes earlier. The calluses will form.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regles-goggins-mental_p28"><h2>The Common Thread</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regles-goggins-mental_p29">These five rules share one principle: <strong>embrace the suck.</strong> Do not run from discomfort. Run toward it. Because on the other side of discomfort is everything you want.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regles-goggins-mental_p30">Goggins did not become mentally tough by accident. He forged it — day after day, choice after choice, discomfort after discomfort.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regles-goggins-mental_p31"><blockquote><em>Who's gonna carry the boats? — David Goggins</em></blockquote></div>
