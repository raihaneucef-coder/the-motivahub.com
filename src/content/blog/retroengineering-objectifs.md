# Reverse Engineering: How to Work Backward from Your Goal
Most people set a goal and then figure out how to get there. The most successful people do the opposite — they start at the end and work backward.

This is reverse engineering, and it is the fastest path from where you are to where you want to be.

## How Reverse Engineering Works

Instead of asking "How do I achieve this goal?" ask "What needs to be true for this goal to be accomplished?"

### Example: Writing a Book

**Goal:** Finish a 60,000-word manuscript in 6 months

**Reverse engineer:**
- 60,000 words in 6 months = 10,000 words per month
- 10,000 words per month = 2,500 words per week
- 2,500 words per week = 500 words per day
- 500 words per day = 1 hour of writing

The goal becomes a daily habit. And daily habits are achievable.

### Example: Saving Money

**Goal:** Save 6000€ in one year

**Reverse engineer:**
- 6000€ in 12 months = 500€ per month
- 500€ per month = 125€ per week
- 125€ per week = 18€ per day

The goal becomes a daily decision. And daily decisions are within your control.

## Why Reverse Engineering Works

### 1. It Makes the Goal Concrete
Instead of a distant, abstract goal, you have a daily action. The daily action is tangible and achievable.

### 2. It Reveals the Path
Working backward shows you exactly what needs to happen — and when. There is no ambiguity.

### 3. It Eliminates Overwhelm
A big goal feels overwhelming. A daily action does not. Reverse engineering breaks the big goal into small, manageable pieces.

### 4. It Creates Accountability
When you know exactly what you need to do each day, you cannot hide from the work. The plan is clear. The execution is up to you.

## How to Reverse Engineer Any Goal

1. Define the goal clearly
2. Set a deadline
3. Work backward from the deadline to today
4. Identify the daily action required
5. Execute the daily action without fail
