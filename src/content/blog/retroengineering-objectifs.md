<div data-i18n-html="article_retroengineering-objectifs_p5"><h2>How Reverse Engineering Works</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_retroengineering-objectifs_p6">Instead of asking "How do I achieve this goal?" ask "What needs to be true for this goal to be accomplished?"</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_retroengineering-objectifs_p7"><h3>Example: Writing a Book</h3></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_retroengineering-objectifs_p8"><strong>Goal:</strong> Finish a 60,000-word manuscript in 6 months</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_retroengineering-objectifs_p9"><strong>Reverse engineer:</strong>
- 60,000 words in 6 months = 10,000 words per month
- 10,000 words per month = 2,500 words per week
- 2,500 words per week = 500 words per day
- 500 words per day = 1 hour of writing</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_retroengineering-objectifs_p10">The goal becomes a daily habit. And daily habits are achievable.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_retroengineering-objectifs_p11"><h3>Example: Saving Money</h3></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_retroengineering-objectifs_p12"><strong>Goal:</strong> Save 6000€ in one year</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_retroengineering-objectifs_p13"><strong>Reverse engineer:</strong>
- 6000€ in 12 months = 500€ per month
- 500€ per month = 125€ per week
- 125€ per week = 18€ per day</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_retroengineering-objectifs_p14">The goal becomes a daily decision. And daily decisions are within your control.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_retroengineering-objectifs_p15"><h2>Why Reverse Engineering Works</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_retroengineering-objectifs_p16"><h3>1. It Makes the Goal Concrete</h3>
Instead of a distant, abstract goal, you have a daily action. The daily action is tangible and achievable.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_retroengineering-objectifs_p17"><h3>2. It Reveals the Path</h3>
Working backward shows you exactly what needs to happen — and when. There is no ambiguity.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_retroengineering-objectifs_p18"><h3>3. It Eliminates Overwhelm</h3>
A big goal feels overwhelming. A daily action does not. Reverse engineering breaks the big goal into small, manageable pieces.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_retroengineering-objectifs_p19"><h3>4. It Creates Accountability</h3>
When you know exactly what you need to do each day, you cannot hide from the work. The plan is clear. The execution is up to you.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_retroengineering-objectifs_p20"><h2>How to Reverse Engineer Any Goal</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_retroengineering-objectifs_p21">1. Define the goal clearly
2. Set a deadline
3. Work backward from the deadline to today
4. Identify the daily action required
5. Execute the daily action without fail</div>
