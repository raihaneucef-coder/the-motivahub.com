# Investing for Beginners: Start Building Wealth Today
The best time to start investing was 20 years ago. The second best time is today. Investing is how you make your money work for you — instead of you working for your money.

## Why You Should Invest

- **Inflation eats savings:** If your money earns 1% in a savings account but inflation is 3%, you are losing 2% per year
- **Compound interest:** The most powerful force in finance. 500€ per month at 8% for 30 years = 745,000€
- **Financial freedom:** Investing is how you build wealth that does not depend on your salary

## How to Start

### 1. Build an Emergency Fund First
Before investing, have 3-6 months of expenses saved. This prevents you from needing to sell investments during an emergency.

### 2. Start with Index Funds
Index funds are baskets of stocks that track the market. They are diversified, low-cost, and require no expertise. Examples: S&P 500 index fund, Total Market index fund.

### 3. Invest Regularly
Set up automatic monthly investments. This is called dollar-cost averaging — you buy more when prices are low and less when prices are high.

### 4. Keep Costs Low
Choose funds with low expense ratios (under 0.5%). High fees destroy returns over time.

### 5. Stay the Course
The market will go up and down. Do not panic-sell during dips. Long-term investing wins.

## Common Mistakes

- Waiting for the "right time" — it does not exist
- Trying to time the market — even professionals fail at this
- Checking your portfolio daily — it creates anxiety
- Investing money you need in the short term — only invest money you can leave for 5+ years

> *The stock market is a device for transferring money from the impatient to the patient. — Warren Buffett*
