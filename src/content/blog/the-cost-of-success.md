We love the highlight. We rarely ask what it cost when the cameras were off.

Every success you admire was paid for in advance, in a currency most people refuse to spend: boredom, repetition, and saying no when yes would have been more fun.

**The cost is not paid once. It is paid daily, in small coins.**

The writer who finished the book paid with mornings when the page stayed blank. The athlete who stood on the podium paid with nights when sleep mattered more than the party. The business that looks overnight paid with years when nothing looked like progress.

We want the reward without the rent. But the rent is the work.

Ask before you chase the next success: what is this one asking me to give up, and am I willing to give it not once, but for a year?

If the answer is yes, the price stops feeling like a punishment and starts feeling like proof. You are not losing something. You are buying the person you want to become.

Success is not free. It is just worth the cost — if you choose the cost on purpose.
