Cal Newport defined deep work as "professional activities performed in a state of distraction-free concentration that push your cognitive capabilities to their limit." It is the skill that separates the best from the rest.

In a world full of noise, deep work is your unfair advantage.

## Why Deep Work Matters

The economy rewards two types of people: those who can work with intelligent machines, and those who are the best at what they do. Both require deep work.

Shallow work — emails, meetings, administrative tasks — keeps you busy. Deep work creates value. The problem is that most people spend all day in shallow mode and wonder why they are not progressing.

## How to Build Deep Work

### 1. Schedule It
Block 2-4 hours every day for deep work. Put it in your calendar. Treat it like a meeting with your most important client — because your most important client is your future self.

### 2. Eliminate Distractions
Phone on airplane mode. All notifications off. No email. No social media. No interruptions. If you cannot eliminate all distractions, eliminate as many as possible.

### 3. Start Small
If you have never done deep work, start with 25 minutes (one Pomodoro). Then increase to 45 minutes. Then 90 minutes. Your focus muscle needs training.

### 4. Track It
Use our [Habit Tracker](/tracker/) to mark every day you complete a deep work session. The visual proof of consistency is its own reward.

## The Deep Work Schedule

The best time for deep work is the morning, when your willpower is highest. Protect your morning for deep work. Save shallow work for the afternoon.

> *The ability to perform deep work is becoming increasingly rare at exactly the same time it is becoming increasingly valuable. — Cal Newport*
