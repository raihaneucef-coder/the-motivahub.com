<div data-i18n-html="article_deep-work-focus_p2">Deep work is the ability to focus without distraction on a cognitively demanding task. Learn how to develop this rare skill and multiply your output.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_deep-work-focus_p3">Cal Newport defined deep work as "professional activities performed in a state of distraction-free concentration that push your cognitive capabilities to their limit." It is the skill that separates the best from the rest.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_deep-work-focus_p4">In a world full of noise, deep work is your unfair advantage.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_deep-work-focus_p5"><h2>Why Deep Work Matters</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_deep-work-focus_p6">The economy rewards two types of people: those who can work with intelligent machines, and those who are the best at what they do. Both require deep work.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_deep-work-focus_p7">Shallow work — emails, meetings, administrative tasks — keeps you busy. Deep work creates value. The problem is that most people spend all day in shallow mode and wonder why they are not progressing.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_deep-work-focus_p8"><h2>How to Build Deep Work</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_deep-work-focus_p9"><h3>1. Schedule It</h3>
Block 2-4 hours every day for deep work. Put it in your calendar. Treat it like a meeting with your most important client — because your most important client is your future self.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_deep-work-focus_p10"><h3>2. Eliminate Distractions</h3>
Phone on airplane mode. All notifications off. No email. No social media. No interruptions. If you cannot eliminate all distractions, eliminate as many as possible.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_deep-work-focus_p11"><h3>3. Start Small</h3>
If you have never done deep work, start with 25 minutes (one Pomodoro). Then increase to 45 minutes. Then 90 minutes. Your focus muscle needs training.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_deep-work-focus_p12"><h3>4. Track It</h3>
Use our <a href="/tracker/">Habit Tracker</a> to mark every day you complete a deep work session. The visual proof of consistency is its own reward.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_deep-work-focus_p13"><h2>The Deep Work Schedule</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_deep-work-focus_p14">The best time for deep work is the morning, when your willpower is highest. Protect your morning for deep work. Save shallow work for the afternoon.</div>
