There are two types of goals: outcome goals and process goals. Outcome goals are what you want to achieve. Process goals are what you do every day to get there.

Most people focus on outcomes. The most successful people focus on processes.

## The Difference

**Outcome Goal:** "I want to lose 10 kg"
**Process Goal:** "I will eat 1800 calories and exercise 30 minutes daily"

**Outcome Goal:** "I want to get promoted"
**Process Goal:** "I will deliver my best work every day and ask for feedback weekly"

**Outcome Goal:** "I want to write a book"
**Process Goal:** "I will write 500 words every morning"

The outcome is not fully in your control. The process is. And focusing on what you can control produces better results than obsessing over what you cannot.

## Why Process Goals Win

### 1. They Are Actionable
You can do a process goal right now. An outcome goal might take months or years.

### 2. They Build Habits
Process goals, repeated daily, become habits. And habits are the foundation of all achievement.

### 3. They Reduce Anxiety
Focusing on outcomes creates anxiety — there are too many variables. Focusing on processes creates calm — you know exactly what to do.

### 4. They Create Consistency
Process goals are repeatable. You can do them every day, regardless of external circumstances.

## How to Set Process Goals

1. Define your outcome goal
2. Ask: "What daily action will move me toward this outcome?"
3. Make the action specific and measurable
4. Commit to doing it every day
5. Track your consistency
