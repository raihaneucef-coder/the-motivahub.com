<div data-i18n-html="article_jeune-intermittent_p2">Intermittent fasting is the most popular eating pattern in the world. But does it actually deliver results? The science says yes — with a caveat.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_jeune-intermittent_p3">Intermittent fasting (IF) is not a diet — it is an eating pattern. You cycle between periods of eating and fasting. The most common method is 16:8 — fast for 16 hours, eat for 8.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_jeune-intermittent_p4"><h2>What the Science Says</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_jeune-intermittent_p5"><h3>1. Weight Loss</h3>
IF reduces calorie intake naturally. When you eat in a shorter window, you tend to eat less. This creates a calorie deficit — the only requirement for weight loss.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_jeune-intermittent_p6"><h3>2. Insulin Sensitivity</h3>
Fasting lowers insulin levels, which improves insulin sensitivity. This reduces the risk of type 2 diabetes.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_jeune-intermittent_p7"><h3>3. Cellular Repair</h3>
Fasting triggers autophagy — your body's cellular cleaning process. This removes damaged cells and promotes renewal.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_jeune-intermittent_p8"><h3>4. Brain Health</h3>
Fasting increases BDNF (brain-derived neurotrophic factor), which supports brain health and cognitive function.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_jeune-intermittent_p9"><h3>5. Longevity</h3>
Animal studies show that fasting extends lifespan. Human studies are ongoing, but early results are promising.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_jeune-intermittent_p10"><h2>Common Mistakes</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_jeune-intermittent_p11"><h3>1. Eating Too Much During the Eating Window</h3>
IF is not a license to eat junk food. You still need to eat nutritious meals.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_jeune-intermittent_p12"><h3>2. Not Drinking Enough Water</h3>
Fasting does not mean dehydrating. Drink plenty of water, black coffee, or tea during your fasting window.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_jeune-intermittent_p13"><h3>3. Starting Too Aggressively</h3>
Start with 12:12 (fast 12 hours, eat 12 hours) and gradually increase to 16:8.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_jeune-intermittent_p14"><h3>4. Ignoring Your Body</h3>
IF is not for everyone. If you feel dizzy, weak, or unwell, break your fast. Listen to your body.</div>
