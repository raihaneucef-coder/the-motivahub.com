Maria taught in a small school in a poor neighborhood. Her salary was barely enough to cover rent. She had no car, no vacation home, no investment portfolio. By the world's standards, she was not successful.

But Maria changed 1000 lives.

## The Ripple Effect

Every year, Maria took on 30 new students. She did not just teach them math and reading. She taught them to believe in themselves. She told them they were smart. She told them they mattered. She told them they could be anything they wanted to be.

Many of her students became teachers, doctors, engineers, and business owners. They all credit Maria. She gave them the one thing no textbook could: belief.

## The Lesson

Impact is not measured in money. It is measured in lives changed. Maria never became rich. But she became irreplaceable. She created a legacy that will outlive her by generations.

## The Choice

You have the same choice Maria had. You can pursue wealth, or you can pursue impact. You can pursue fame, or you can pursue meaning. You can pursue success, or you can pursue significance.

The most fulfilled people pursue significance.
