Every time you switch between different types of tasks, your brain pays a cognitive tax. It takes time to refocus, reorient, and get back into flow. Batching eliminates this tax.

## What Is Batching?

Batching means doing all similar tasks at once instead of spreading them throughout the day.

Instead of:
- Check email at 9 AM, then work on project, then check email at 11 AM, then work on project

Do this:
- Check all email from 9:00-9:30
- Work on project from 9:30-12:00

The result is less time lost to context switching and more time in deep work.

## Types of Batches

### Communication Batch
- Check email 2-3 times per day at set times
- Return all phone calls in one block
- Respond to messages in a single batch

### Creative Batch
- Write all your articles in one sitting
- Record all your videos in one session
- Design all your graphics in one block

### Administrative Batch
- Do all your invoicing at once
- File all your documents in one session
- Complete all your reports in one block

### Errand Batch
- Do all your shopping in one trip
- Run all your errands in one block
- Complete all your calls in one session

## Why Batching Works

1. **Reduces context switching** — your brain stays in one mode
2. **Increases speed** — you get faster at repetitive tasks
3. **Improves quality** — focus on one type of task produces better results
4. **Saves energy** — less mental overhead from switching

> *Batching is not about doing more. It is about doing less — but doing it better.*
