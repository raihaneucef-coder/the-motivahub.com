<div data-i18n-html="article_regarder-intentionnellement_p2">Not all entertainment is created equal. Some movies and shows inspire, educate, and transform. Learn how to watch with intention.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regarder-intentionnellement_p3">Entertainment is not inherently bad. It becomes bad when it is mindless. When you watch with intention, entertainment can be educational, inspiring, and even life-changing.</div>


<div data-i18n-html="article_regarder-intentionnellement_p4"><h2>Mindful Entertainment</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regarder-intentionnellement_p5"><h3>1. Choose with Purpose</h3>
<a href="/journal/the-movie-that-changed-your-week/">Before pressing play</a>, name what you actually want: inspiration, a good cry, a laugh, real rest, one specific idea. Naming the reason is the difference between picking a film and letting the algorithm pick one for you. If you cannot answer in thirty seconds, close the app — the want is not there yet, and forcing a watch becomes another scroll.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regarder-intentionnellement_p6"><h3>2. Watch Actively</h3>
Put the phone face-down. Not for purity — a second screen turns the film into wallpaper and you will not remember it in a week. Notice the moment a character choice surprises you, the line you wanted to say out loud, the scene you wished ran longer. Those three data points are usually enough to carry the whole film with you.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regarder-intentionnellement_p7"><h3>3. Discuss What You Watch</h3>
Within twenty-four hours, say one sentence out loud to someone: what the film was actually about for you. Not the plot — the meaning. Turning a fuzzy impression into language is the difference between having seen something and having taken something from it. If nobody is around, write the sentence in a note; the effect is close enough.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regarder-intentionnellement_p8"><h3>4. Apply What You Learn</h3>
Every story that moves you points at a value you are underfeeding. Take one concrete step within a week or admit the reaction was only mood. Call the parent, book the trip, cancel the thing, start the draft. <a href="/journal/play-on-purpose/">Entertainment you act on earns its two hours</a>; entertainment you do not is a very long advert.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regarder-intentionnellement_p9"><h2>Movies That Can Change Your Life</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regarder-intentionnellement_p10">- <strong>The Pursuit of Happyness</strong> — persistence through adversity
- <strong>Dead Poets Society</strong> — the courage to think differently
- <strong>The Shawshank Redemption</strong> — hope and patience
- <strong>Into the Wild</strong> — the search for meaning
- <strong>Inception</strong> — the power of ideas</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regarder-intentionnellement_p11"><h2>The Balance</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_regarder-intentionnellement_p12">Entertainment is like sugar — enjoyable in moderation, harmful in excess. Set limits on screen time and choose content that <a href="/journal/play-on-purpose/">adds value to your life</a>.</div>
