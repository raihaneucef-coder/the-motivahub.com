<div data-i18n-html="article_confiance-inbranlable_p5"><h2>The 30-Day Confidence Plan</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_confiance-inbranlable_p6"><h3>Week 1: Foundations</h3>
- <strong>Day 1:</strong> Write down 3 things you are good at
- <strong>Day 2:</strong> Make eye contact with 5 strangers
- <strong>Day 3:</strong> Speak up in a meeting or group conversation
- <strong>Day 4:</strong> Wear something that makes you feel powerful
- <strong>Day 5:</strong> Do something that scares you slightly
- <strong>Day 6:</strong> Give a genuine compliment to someone
- <strong>Day 7:</strong> Review your week and note what worked</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_confiance-inbranlable_p7"><h3>Week 2: Expanding</h3>
- <strong>Day 8:</strong> Start a conversation with a stranger
- <strong>Day 9:</strong> Share an opinion you normally keep to yourself
- <strong>Day 10:</strong> Do something you have been putting off
- <strong>Day 11:</strong> Ask for feedback from someone you respect
- <strong>Day 12:</strong> Take on a responsibility you normally avoid
- <strong>Day 13:</strong> Say "no" to something you do not want to do
- <strong>Day 14:</strong> Review your progress</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_confiance-inbranlable_p8"><h3>Week 3: Strengthening</h3>
- <strong>Day 15:</strong> Take a leadership role in something
- <strong>Day 16:</strong> Share a personal story publicly
- <strong>Day 17:</strong> Do something that requires courage
- <strong>Day 18:</strong> Accept a compliment without deflecting
- <strong>Day 19:</strong> Set a boundary you have been avoiding
- <strong>Day 20:</strong> Celebrate a win — no matter how small
- <strong>Day 21:</strong> Review your progress</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_confiance-inbranlable_p9"><h3>Week 4: Solidifying</h3>
- <strong>Day 22:</strong> Teach something you know well
- <strong>Day 23:</strong> Ask for what you want directly
- <strong>Day 24:</strong> Take full responsibility for something
- <strong>Day 25:</strong> Do something that matters to you, regardless of others' opinions
- <strong>Day 26:</strong> Share your work with the world
- <strong>Day 27:</strong> Do something uncomfortable on purpose
- <strong>Day 28:</strong> Reflect on how far you have come
- <strong>Day 29:</strong> Write a letter to your future confident self
- <strong>Day 30:</strong> Celebrate your transformation</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_confiance-inbranlable_p10"><h2>The Science of Confidence</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_confiance-inbranlable_p11">Confidence is built through evidence. Every time you do something that scares you and survive, you create evidence that you are capable. Over time, this evidence builds an unshakable belief in yourself.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_confiance-inbranlable_p12"><blockquote><em>Confidence is not "they will like me." Confidence is "I will be fine if they do not."</em></blockquote></div>
