The typical tourist checks off 5 countries in 2 weeks. They take photos at every landmark, eat at every chain restaurant, and return home more exhausted than when they left.

The slow traveler spends 2 weeks in one place. They learn the neighborhood, eat at local spots, and leave with real memories — not just photos.

## What Is Slow Travel?

Slow travel is traveling to fewer places for longer periods. Instead of rushing from one attraction to the next, you immerse yourself in one place. You live like a local, not a tourist.

## Why Slow Travel Is Better

### 1. Deeper Connections
When you stay in one place, you meet people. You learn names. You build relationships. These connections are the real souvenirs of travel.

### 2. Less Stress
No early morning flights. No packed itineraries. No rushing from one place to the next. Slow travel is relaxing.

### 3. More Authentic
You discover the hidden gems — the local restaurant, the quiet park, the neighborhood market — that tourists never see.

### 4. Cheaper
Staying in one place is cheaper than constantly moving. You can negotiate weekly rates, cook at home, and avoid transportation costs.

### 5. Better for the Environment
Fewer flights = lower carbon footprint. Slow travel is sustainable travel.

## How to Travel Slowly

1. Choose one city instead of three
2. Stay in an apartment instead of a hotel
3. Shop at local markets instead of restaurants
4. Walk instead of taking taxis
5. Learn a few phrases in the local language
