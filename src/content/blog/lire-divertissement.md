Reading is the most underrated form of entertainment. It is free (or nearly free), portable, and it improves your brain while you enjoy it. No other entertainment can claim that.

## Why Reading Is Better Than Screens

### 1. It Builds Focus
Reading requires sustained attention for 20-60 minutes. This trains your brain to focus — a skill that is increasingly rare.

### 2. It Expands Vocabulary
Readers encounter 50% more vocabulary than non-readers. Better vocabulary means better communication.

### 3. It Increases Empathy
Reading fiction puts you inside other people's lives. This builds empathy and emotional intelligence.

### 4. It Reduces Stress
A study by the University of Sussex found that reading reduces stress by 68%. It is more effective than listening to music or drinking tea.

### 5. It Improves Sleep
Reading a physical book before bed signals your brain to produce melatonin. Screens do the opposite.

## How to Read More

1. Start with 10 pages per day
2. Always carry a book
3. Read before bed instead of scrolling
4. Join a book club for accountability
5. Choose books that genuinely interest you — not books you think you should read
