<div data-i18n-html="article_lire-divertissement_p3">Reading is the most underrated form of entertainment. It is free (or nearly free), portable, and it improves your brain while you enjoy it. No other entertainment can claim that.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_lire-divertissement_p4"><h2>Why Reading Is Better Than Screens</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_lire-divertissement_p5"><h3>1. It Builds Focus</h3>
Reading requires sustained attention for 20-60 minutes. This trains your brain to focus — a skill that is increasingly rare.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_lire-divertissement_p6"><h3>2. It Expands Vocabulary</h3>
Readers encounter 50% more vocabulary than non-readers. Better vocabulary means better communication.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_lire-divertissement_p7"><h3>3. It Increases Empathy</h3>
Reading fiction puts you inside other people's lives. This builds empathy and emotional intelligence.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_lire-divertissement_p8"><h3>4. It Reduces Stress</h3>
A study by the University of Sussex found that reading reduces stress by 68%. It is more effective than listening to music or drinking tea.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_lire-divertissement_p9"><h3>5. It Improves Sleep</h3>
Reading a physical book before bed signals your brain to produce melatonin. Screens do the opposite.</div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_lire-divertissement_p10"><h2>How to Read More</h2></div>

<div data-i18n-html="article_lire-divertissement_p11">1. Start with 10 pages per day
2. Always carry a book
3. Read before bed instead of scrolling
4. Join a book club for accountability
5. Choose books that genuinely interest you — not books you think you should read</div>
