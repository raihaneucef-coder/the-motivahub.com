# Attachment Styles: Why You Love the Way You Do
Have you ever wondered why some people pull away when things get close, while others cling desperately? The answer lies in your attachment style — a pattern of relating to others that forms in childhood.

## The 4 Attachment Styles

### 1. Secure
You are comfortable with intimacy and independence. You trust easily, communicate openly, and do not fear abandonment. About 56% of people have a secure attachment style.

### 2. Anxious-Preoccupied
You crave intimacy but fear abandonment. You need constant reassurance, worry about your partner's feelings, and become clingy under stress. About 20% of people have this style.

### 3. Dismissive-Avoidant
You value independence over intimacy. You suppress your feelings, pull away when things get close, and struggle with vulnerability. About 25% of people have this style.

### 4. Fearful-Avoidant
You want intimacy but fear it. You push people away when they get close, then pull them back when they leave. This is the most chaotic attachment style. About 1% of people have this style.

## Can You Change Your Attachment Style?

Yes. Attachment styles are not fixed. With awareness, therapy, and practice, you can develop a secure attachment style. The first step is recognizing your current style.

## How to Build Secure Attachment

1. Learn your style — understand your patterns
2. Communicate your needs — do not expect others to guess
3. Practice vulnerability — share your feelings even when it is scary
4. Build trust through consistency — show up reliably
5. Seek therapy if needed — a professional can help you rewrite your patterns
